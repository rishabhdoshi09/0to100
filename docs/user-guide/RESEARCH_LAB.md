# Research Lab (testing ideas honestly)

The Research Lab studies **history** to check whether an idea really worked. It makes
**no trades** — not even paper ones. It only studies.

## The three possible results
- **PASS** — *promising* under the registered test. **Not** a promise of profit.
- **FAIL** — the idea did not meet the required test. (This is useful — it saves you
  from a losing idea.)
- **INCONCLUSIVE** — not enough trustworthy evidence to say pass or fail.

## A special case: DATA_UNAVAILABLE
Sometimes the historical data needed for a test is **not installed**. Then the system
says **INCONCLUSIVE — DATA UNAVAILABLE** instead of guessing. A made-up verdict would
be dishonest.

## Why a PASS is not a green light
- History is not the future.
- A test can look good by luck; the Lab uses strict checks to reduce that, but cannot
  remove it.
- Real money needs *forward* evidence and a formal sign-off, not just a historical PASS.

## Why we keep FAILs
A clean FAIL is progress: it rules out an idea and protects your money and time.
