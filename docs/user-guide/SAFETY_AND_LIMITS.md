# Safety and limits

These protect your account. They **cannot be quietly bypassed**.

## The daily safety stop (circuit breaker)
After losses reach a set amount for the day, the system **stops trading for the day**.
No "making it back". It resets on the next trading day. If you see **"Daily safety stop
active"**, you are done for today — review your trades and rest.

## The daily trade limit
There is a maximum number of trades per day. Once you hit it, even a great-looking setup
is **skipped on purpose**. This stops over-trading.

## Risk per trade
Each trade risks only a small, fixed part of the account. The **maximum possible loss**
is shown before you commit.

## The live lock (very important)
Real-money (LIVE) trading is held behind a **temporary lock**. Turning on an internal
switch does **not** make a strategy safe or eligible. Live needs, at minimum: forward
practice evidence, a portfolio-level result, broker-record checks, and a formal sign-off.
Until then, LIVE stays off.

## Changing a safety setting
Any safety-sensitive change shows a **specific** message, for example:
> "You are changing the daily PAPER trade limit from 3 to 5. This permits up to two
> additional paper trades per NSE trading day. It does not enable LIVE trading."

You always see exactly what changes, what it affects (paper/live), and how to reverse it.
