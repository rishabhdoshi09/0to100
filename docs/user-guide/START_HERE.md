# Start Here — QuantTerm in plain words

**QuantTerm is a careful assistant for the stock market.** It studies the market,
practises trades with *pretend* money, and only ever *suggests*. It does not gamble
your real money.

## The one-minute version
- It looks at Indian stocks and finds *possible* setups.
- It checks each one against strict rules and tells you **why** it said yes, no, or wait.
- It can **practise** with pretend money so you learn safely.
- It can **study history** to test whether an idea really worked (Research).
- Real-money trading is **switched off on purpose** right now.

## What it will NOT promise
- It cannot promise profit. Markets are uncertain.
- A good research result (PASS) is **promising, not a promise**.
- Practice profit is **not** proof you'll profit for real.
- It will **not** give you a trade every day. Most days there is nothing worth doing.
- It cannot protect you from risking money you can't afford to lose.

## The three modes (remember these)
- **Research** — studies old data. Makes no trades at all.
- **Paper** — practises with pretend money. Nothing reaches a real account.
- **Live** — real money. **Locked** until strict safety and evidence rules are met.

## Your first five minutes
1. Open **Simple Home**. Read the big status line.
2. Do the **one** "Next best action" it suggests.
3. Open **Getting Started** for the 7-step tour.
4. Do the **Practice Walkthrough** (a safe, made-up example).
5. Keep the **Glossary** handy for any scary word.
