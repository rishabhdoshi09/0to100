## Does expectancy deteriorate as entry gets farther from the pivot?

**Not estimable as a slope.** Widths ≤1.0% produced zero fills. Widening to 5% added **one extra loser**, not a stable positive region.

What *is* identified: most unique VCPs are already **>1.5%** through last-contraction resistance at first snapshot (median **+10.7%**, 75% extended). That is a **timing / lifecycle** fact, not a reason to move the buy-zone to 5–10%.

Do not widen the zone to manufacture trades.
