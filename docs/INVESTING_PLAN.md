## The plan

### 1. Core = low-cost, broad index (the engine)
The bulk of the equity allocation sits in **broad-market index funds/ETFs** chosen
on ONE hard rule: **lowest total cost + lowest tracking error + large AUM.**

- **Nifty 50** index fund/ETF — the core large-cap block.
- **Nifty Next 50** (optional) — a growth tilt on the next-largest names.
- OR a single **Nifty 500 / total-market** index fund for one-fund simplicity.
- Prefer **direct plans** (no distributor commission) and **expense ratio ≤ ~0.2%**.
  A 1% higher fee compounds into a huge drag over decades — costs are the one thing
  you fully control (EXP-002's whole lesson).

### 2. Asset allocation (risk lives here, not in stock-picking)
Your real risk control is the **equity / debt / gold split**, not clever signals.
A simple, common framework — pick the row matching your horizon & stomach:

| Profile | Equity (index) | Debt | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggressive (10y+ horizon) | 75% | 15% | 10% |
| Moderate | 60% | 30% | 10% |
| Conservative | 40% | 50% | 10% |

- **Debt:** EPF/PPF (safe + tax-efficient), or a liquid/short-duration fund for the
  flexible part. This is your "sleep-at-night" and crash-buying reserve.
- **Gold:** Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) preferred (interest + no expense) or a gold
  ETF — a small hedge, not a bet.

### 3. Discipline mechanism = automated SIP
- Set a **monthly SIP** into the core and forget it. Automation removes the emotion
  that made 93% lose.
- **Rebalance once a year** back to your target split — nothing more. (Selling what
  ran, buying what lagged: a free, mechanical edge.)
- **Increase the SIP as income grows.** Time in market + rising contributions is the
  actual wealth engine — not returns cleverness.

### 4. Behavioural guardrails (the REAL edge)
These are what separate the 7% from the 93%:
- ❌ **No F&O, no intraday, no tips/influencer trades.** SEBI proved where that ends.
- ❌ **Don't check daily. Don't react to news.** A plan you tinker with isn't a plan.
- ❌ **Never stop the SIP (or withdraw) in a crash** — that's when you're buying
  cheap. Crashes are features, not bugs, for a long-horizon SIP.
- ✅ **Costs, discipline, time.** That's the whole game.

## Where the system you built still earns its keep

QuantTerm doesn't retire — its job changes from "find trades" to **"guard the
truth."**

1. **The gauntlet is your proof-gate.** Any active idea — a factor tilt, a sector
   bet, anything — gets **ZERO real money** until it PASSes the gauntlet on
   **survivorship-free, CA-adjusted data.** EXP-005 is the permanent reminder of
   why: biased data will happily hand you a 37% mirage.
2. **A capped satellite (optional, ≤10%).** If — and only if — an idea earns a
   clean PASS *and* months of tracked paper evidence (`signal_log`), you may run it
   as a small satellite around the index core. Until then: **100% core.**
3. **Paper-trade to build real evidence.** Keep the app running in paper mode; let
   `signal_log` accumulate genuine forward outcomes. That's the only honest way an
   edge ever graduates.
4. **`RESEARCH_LOG.md` is your decision journal.** Every future idea gets
   pre-registered and recorded — win or lose. Negative evidence is as valuable as
   positive.
